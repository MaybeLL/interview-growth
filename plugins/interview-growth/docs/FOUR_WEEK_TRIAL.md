# Four-week product trial

The trial tests one claim: focused practice should close at least one target-role capability gap,
and that improvement should survive an independent retest on a different question.

## Before starting

- Choose one target role and keep it as an isolated goal.
- Add the target JD and constraints, review the generated standard, and approve it.
- Create a backup before the first interview.
- Reserve two 45-minute sessions per week.

## Week 1 — Establish a baseline

Run enough formal mock interviews to collect at least three independent attempts across two or more
questions for the most important requirements. Do not coach during these attempts. Review the
dashboard and select one critical or high-impact gap with sufficient evidence.

Record:

- starting requirement level and coverage;
- the exact evidence and omissions behind the gap;
- whether any evaluation needed dispute and blind reassessment.

## Week 2 — Execute one prescription

Create one training prescription with an observable action, such as explaining failure recovery
with explicit latency and consistency trade-offs. Use coached practice freely, but persist it as
`hinted` or `coached`; it must not change readiness.

At the end of the week, schedule a formal retest using a question that has not contributed eligible
evidence for that gap.

## Week 3 — Retest independently

Run the scheduled retest without hints, examples, notes, or answer editing. Record the raw answer
before evaluation. If the result remains below target, revise the prescription rather than
relabeling coached work as evidence.

The gap counts as provisionally closed only when current level and evidence coverage both meet the
approved standard.

## Week 4 — Test transfer to reality

After a real interview, use `real-interview-review` to record recalled questions, answer summaries,
feedback, and the round result. Review `blind_spot` and `unmapped` questions; prepare new simulated
coverage where useful. Real-interview memories remain non-scoring.

Create a final backup and `.igx` export. Compare the starting and ending dashboard explanations.

## Success criteria

- At least one important gap closes on a different-question independent retest.
- Most prescriptions are concrete enough to execute without reinterpretation.
- The dashboard can explain both readiness and remaining blockers from traceable evidence.
- Real-interview reviews reveal fewer repeated coverage blind spots over time.
- No coached response, recalled answer, or disputed evaluation enters readiness incorrectly.

If the trial fails, preserve the evidence. Record whether the cause was an inaccurate target
standard, poor question/rubric coverage, evaluator instability, an ineffective prescription, or a
genuine persistent skill gap.
